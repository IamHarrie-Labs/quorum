Bring in your first holder

Two steps. First you register who they are, then you can send them something. In a real rollout, step one happens after they've gone through Cleanverse's identity check; here we skip straight to it to keep the demo moving.

One person can have several wallets. Use the same identifier for all of them and Quorum treats them as one holder, no matter how many addresses they use.

How it works

Connect a wallet

Any wallet works, as long as it's on Monad testnet. You'll only pay gas.

Set your numbers

Pick a preset, or type in your own cap, window, and concentration limit.

Sign once

That one signature sets everything up and makes your wallet the owner.

Nobody else can touch it

Not even us. We tried, on-chain, and got turned away. Here's how.
