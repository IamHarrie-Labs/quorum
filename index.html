Every private placement rule is a counting rule.

Cross one of these numbers and the exemption is void retroactively. Not the last sale. Every sale in the offering becomes an unregistered securities sale, and every investor who bought one acquires a claim against the issuer.

Today the count lives in a spreadsheet a transfer agent updates by hand, reconciled monthly, which means after the transfers have already gone through.

A blockchain counts wallets. The law counts people.

Open fifty wallets in an afternoon and a headcount denominated in addresses stops meaning anything. That is the reason no chain enforces these limits today.

Cleanverse issues a bank verified credential bound to a wallet, and every wallet a person registers under their customer record resolves back to that one record. Quorum reads that binding, collapses wallets into persons, and counts those instead.

Four checks run on every transfer. Three of them are Cleanverse.

Every rule in the Cleanverse engine looks at one wallet at a time: tier, sub tier, group, country. None of them can express an aggregate. Cleanverse asks whether this recipient is allowed. Quorum asks whether the whole holder base still satisfies the law once this transfer settles.

Cleanverse A-Token rule

Is this wallet eligible?

Verification tier and jurisdiction, checked against the holder's A-Pass country tags.

Cleanverse Validator pool

Is this contract permitted?

Quorum registers itself as a compliance pool with Cleanverse, so a check on this register runs the same way a check on any other registered pool would.

Quorum, enforced by Cleanverse

Has this person been granted a seat?

Quorum computes the aggregate that the Cleanverse engine has no way to compute, then writes the result back into the person's credential as a subgroup tag. A rule requiring that tag means an unseated wallet fails Cleanverse's own check, not only ours. Identity goes in, seat state comes out, and the enforcement happens in their engine.

Quorum set invariant

Would the room still be legal afterwards?

Headcount over a rolling window, and a concentration ceiling measured per person across every wallet they control. Proven under arbitrary sequences of mints, transfers and revocations, not just the sequence in the demo.
